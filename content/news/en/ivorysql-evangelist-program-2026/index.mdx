<p>
  If you've written about IvorySQL or PostgreSQL, answered questions in the community, participated in events, or simply love open source — you're exactly who we're looking for.
</p>
<p>
  The IvorySQL community is launching the <strong>2026 Evangelist Program</strong> today. It's not a full-time job. It's a way to contribute on your own terms, help others discover open source databases, and gain official recognition and unique opportunities.
</p>

<h2 id="benefits">What's in it for you?</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Official recognition</strong>: Digital certificate, exclusive Evangelist badge, and your name on the official "Evangelist Wall" at ivorysql.org</li>
  <li><strong>Premium benefits</strong> (Advanced Evangelists, 300+ points): Physical badge, website feature interview, VIP ticket to HOW 2027, and a chance to attend pgconf.dev</li>
  <li><strong>Annual Recognition Awards</strong>: Best Evangelist of the Year — Trophy + permanent featured spot on the Evangelist Wall; Best Article/Video of the Year — Digital certificate + special mention on our blog and social media</li>
  <li><strong>Exclusive swag bag</strong>: HOW 2026 T-shirt, elephant plushie, and other community goodies</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="who">Who can apply?</h2>
<p>You're eligible if you meet any of these:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Used IvorySQL or PostgreSQL</li>
  <li>Written technical articles about them</li>
  <li>Answered questions in the community</li>
  <li>Participated in online/offline events</li>
  <li>Submitted suggestions, docs, or code</li>
  <li>Love open source and want to learn</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Notes</strong>:</p>
<ol>
  <li>You must register before participating in the Evangelist Program.</li>
  <li>The points rules above were updated on October 30, 2026 and take effect once published. Previously approved points are retained and will not be recalculated retroactively because of these rule changes.</li>
  <li>If multiple people report the same bug, only the first submitter receives points.</li>
  <li>The HOW 2026 speaker bonus is awarded once per person at 30 points. It cannot be combined with other speaking or sharing points for the same talk. Previously approved points remain subject to the historical-points retention principle.</li>
  <li>Apply each promotion subcategory’s own annual cap first, then combine the four promotion subcategories, subject to a maximum of 60 points per person per year. A single promotion record cannot be scored under multiple promotion categories. Once a cap is reached, excess contributions do not add effective points.</li>
  <li>Articles, standalone videos, Zhihu recommendations, and other promotion are credited by their actual publication date; events by the date held; technical answers by their publication date; Issues by their acceptance date; bugs by their confirmation date; and PRs by their merge date. Complete courses, migration case studies, and other contributions are credited by the date of review confirmation.</li>
</ol>

<h2 id="faq">FAQ</h2>
<p><strong>How do I participate?</strong><br/>Please fill out the form first. All evangelism activities should be coordinated with the community operations team before proceeding.</p>
<p><strong>Is 100 points hard to achieve?</strong><br/>Not at all. 100 points ≈ 3-4 in-depth articles, or 2 articles + 1 talk. Very achievable.</p>
<p><strong>Do PostgreSQL-only articles count?</strong><br/>Yes, but at half the points (15 points). We encourage IvorySQL-focused content.</p>
<p><strong>Can points carry over to 2027?</strong><br/>No. This program is for the 2026 calendar year only.</p>
<p><strong>How do I check my points?</strong><br/>We will periodically open a submission channel for uploading proof of contributions and will verify them on a unified schedule.</p>
