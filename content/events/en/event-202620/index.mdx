IvorySQL participated as an official supporting community in the 2026 Straight to Wuzhen Open Source Competitive Challenge. The IvorySQL project ran from June 15, 2026 at 00:00 to October 25, 2026 at 00:00 (China Standard Time, UTC+8). The competition has ended.

Participants contributed to real open-source projects through feature development, bug fixes, and code improvements. Contributions accepted by project communities were evaluated for their quality and value through the Taiyi platform.

101 developers participated in the IvorySQL project, and the community received the Open Source Pioneer Community award.
