The IvorySQL Campus Tour was held at the School of Software Technology, Zhejiang University, Ningbo Campus, on November 3, 2026. Co-hosted by the China Open Source Database Alliance (COSDA) and the IvorySQL open-source community, the event brought together three technical talks and a hands-on Workshop on PostgreSQL, IvorySQL, and AI applications.

This Chinese-language event featured 德哥 on PostgreSQL extensibility and its ecosystem of forks, 梁翔宇 on IvorySQL's development and innovation, and 怀玉杰 on PostgreSQL, AI, and PGNexus Sandbox experiments. 怀玉杰 and 梁翔宇 also led a PostgreSQL × AI Workshop, helping students create online database environments and use psql for SQL operations and feature verification.

For the full recap, talk summaries, and event photos, visit the [Chinese event page](/zh/community/events/event-202619).
