IvorySQL is participating in the open-source contribution track of the fourth OpenAtom Competition, a rolling competition covering the fourth quarter of 2026. The IvorySQL project runs from October 25, 2026 at 00:00 to December 31, 2026 at 23:59 (China Standard Time, UTC+8).

Developers, students, and open-source enthusiasts can contribute through feature development, bug fixes, code improvements, testing, and related project work. New contributors can start with tasks labeled `good first issue`.

Register through Taiyi, review the project requirements, submit contributions through GitHub, and complete the platform's contribution association or reporting steps. Contributions must be merged within the competition period. Eligibility, contribution evaluation, and cash reward allocation follow the official competition rules.
