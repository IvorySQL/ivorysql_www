#
User Survey | Tell Us About Your IvorySQL v5 Experience

📅 March 20-April 20, 2026
🌍 Online Survey
⏱ About 3 minutes

IvorySQL v5, including versions 5.0, 5.1, and 5.3, has been available for some time. The community would now like to understand real-world usage, product feedback, and future expectations from users who are already using IvorySQL, evaluating it, or closely following the PostgreSQL ecosystem.

Survey Period
March 20, 2026 to April 20, 2026

Format
Online survey, with an estimated completion time of around 3 minutes.

Participants
IvorySQL users, evaluators, and developers interested in PostgreSQL, Oracle compatibility, and open-source database ecosystems.

Benefits
Selected participants will receive IvorySQL gifts and technical books, with extra focus on high-quality feedback and practical suggestions.

## What We'd Like to Learn

01
Current Usage

- Whether you are already using IvorySQL

- Which database version you are using now

- Your main scenarios, such as OLTP, analytics, or AI workloads

02
Feedback on IvorySQL v5

- AI SQL features such as NL2SQL and pg_ai_query

- PostgreSQL capability alignment

- Performance, stability, and vector database experience

03
Future Expectations

- AI-native database capabilities and vector retrieval

- Oracle compatibility improvements

- High availability, cloud-native deployment, and ops tooling

## Survey Rewards

To thank the community for participating, IvorySQL will randomly select participants to receive branded gifts and technical books after the survey closes. High-quality feedback, real-world issues, and practical experience sharing may also receive additional recognition and prizes.
