On June 11, 2026, the first episode of the "PG 30th Anniversary Livestream Series" was successfully held, co-hosted by China PostgreSQL Association, TechTalk, and IvorySQL Community.

The livestream featured five industry experts discussing China's PostgreSQL ecosystem, PG extensions, and the IvorySQL Evangelist Program. The highlight was a roundtable debate on "Where does China's PG rank globally?"
